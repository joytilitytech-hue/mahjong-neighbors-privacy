Mahjong Neighbors Privacy Policy

Developer: Joytility Tech
Effective date: October 10, 2026
Android package: tech.joytility.mahjong

Mahjong Neighbors is an ad-free mahjong game designed for offline play. This policy describes information handled by the app, optional purchases, and this privacy-policy website.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game settings, saved matches, character scores, story progress, tutorial progress, daily free-play usage, and purchase entitlement information in local app storage. You do not need to create a Mahjong Neighbors account.

Optional photo avatars

If you use the photo-avatar feature, you can select an image through Android’s document picker or take a photo using your device’s camera app. Mahjong Neighbors accesses the image you choose or capture to crop, preview, and save your portrait locally. It does not upload your portrait to Joytility Tech, perform AI image conversion, or use it for facial recognition. The app does not require access to your entire photo library.

The saved portrait is kept in app-private storage. Temporary camera images are used during the import process and are deleted after processing or when the avatar screen closes. You can remove the app’s saved portrait using “Delete saved portrait.” Removing that portrait does not delete an original image selected from elsewhere on your device.

Developer collection and sharing

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated account service, or developer-operated cloud-upload service. Game progress and avatar photos are not transmitted to Joytility Tech by the app. Joytility Tech does not sell these app data to advertisers or data brokers. The app does not request location, contacts, or microphone access.

Google Play purchases

Optional one-time purchases, including Unlimited Play and regional rules packs, are processed by Google Play. The app connects to Google Play to retrieve product information, start purchases, check or restore ownership, and acknowledge completed purchases. It stores purchase entitlement information and purchase receipts locally to support access to purchased features.

Google may process account, transaction, device, and other information under its own terms and privacy policy. Joytility Tech does not receive your full payment-card details through the app. Google Play may retain purchase records after you uninstall the app so eligible purchases can be restored.

See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Network access and offline play

Core gameplay, tutorials, saved matches, and photo-avatar processing run on your device. Network access supports Google Play billing and purchase restoration. Product availability, new purchases, and restoration may require an internet connection and Google Play services. The app does not operate its own remote gameplay or photo-storage server.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local app data remain on your device until removed through an available in-app control, Android’s “Clear storage” control, or uninstalling the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress and saved portraits; it does not delete Google’s purchase records or original photos stored outside the app. Android backup is disabled in the app manifest, and Joytility Tech does not provide a cloud backup service.

Support communications

If you contact Joytility Tech, information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems. Please do not post purchase tokens, payment details, private photos, or other sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by Joytility Tech. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and web request details when serving the page under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app has no developer-operated account system or advertising and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Joytility Tech through support, please contact us.

Security

The app uses Android’s app-private storage and system image-selection mechanisms. No method of storage or communication can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data handling change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions or support, contact Joytility Tech at joytilitytech+mahjongsupport@gmail.com.
